Liquid Shader Sphere

A custom GLSL vertex shader deforms the core in real time using simplex noise — a living surface, not a static mesh.

Interactive Satellites

Six orbiting models respond to raycasted hover with a glow and scale, and pulse when clicked.

Cinematic Bloom

UnrealBloomPass plus a custom shader pass add vignette, chromatic aberration, and film grain.

GPU Particle Field

3,000 particles drift through space, animated entirely on the GPU for zero per-frame CPU cost.

🖱️

Mouse & Scroll Camera

The camera drifts with your cursor and dollies through the scene as you scroll — two motion systems, one path.

◈

Dynamic Lighting

Colored point lights orbit independently, catching the wireframe shell and floating rings as they pass.
